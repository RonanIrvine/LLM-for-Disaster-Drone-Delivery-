Constraints:
- Each drone trip must start at or after time zero.
- The arrival time at any node must always be non-negative.
- The starting times of two drones on the same trip must differ with a start time gap t_s.
- Each trip must begin with exactly one outgoing route from the source location.
- Deliveries made by different drones to the same location must be separated by at least the required waiting interval w (service time).
- When a drone travels directly from the source to another location, its arrival time must equal its start time plus the travel duration.
- When a drone travels between two delivery locations, its arrival time must equal the previous arrival time plus travel time and any waiting time (w).
- For every node, the number of incoming routes must equal the number of outgoing routes.
- Each visited node must have an arrival time less than or equal to the maximum allowed time t_max.
- A drone cannot travel from a node back to the same node.
- The total amount of resources delivered to each destination must exactly match that destination's demand.
- The total resources a drone delivers on a trip cannot exceed its carrying capacity.
- The total resources loaded at the source must equal the sum of all resources that will be delivered.
- Delivery nodes have negative loads equal to the amount they receive.
- Load is zero for every drone at any node that is neither the source nor a delivery node.
- The total amount of resources dispatched from the source cannot exceed what is available there.
- Every delivery quantity must be nonnegative.
- A trip must be active if there are outstanding delivery requirements.
- If no resources are delivered during a trip, that trip must be inactive and make no movements.
- Each trip must start after the previous one by at least the specified time gap.
- If a delivery occurs at a node, the delivery time must equal the arrival time.
- If no delivery occurs, the delivery time must be zero.
- When a trip is active, the number of visited nodes must match the number of edges traversed.
- If a node is visited, its successor must correspond to an actual delivery edge (i to j) that the UAV travels.
- If the trip is active, the source node must also have a valid successor along a delivery edge (source to j).
- If a node is not visited, it must not have a successor (value = -1).
- If a trip is active, it must visit at least one delivery location.
- If a trip is active, it must deliver at least one resource.
- The next trip must begin only after the previous trip has returned to the source.
- Every active trip must end by returning once to the source node.
- The total energy used by a drone equals the sum of all distances based on its chosen routes.
- The total energy used by a drone must not exceed its available energy.
- Minimize the total energy used by all drones.
- Then minimize the number of active trips.
